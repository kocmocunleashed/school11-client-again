# School 11 — student yearbook

The user rejected the first corporate-looking direction and asked for a more exciting school identity. The replacement is a student yearbook with expressive mathematical typography. All public content remains Mongolian and the school remains in Ulaanbaatar.

## Palette and type
Sunflower #ffda45, ultramarine #2344df, ink #19233b, pink paper #f5a1bd, lilac #eeedff, white paper #faf9f5. Golos Text provides Cyrillic display and body text. PT Serif appears sparingly in mathematical notation and longer inherited reading passages.

## Signature
An oversized dimensional 11 responds gently to mouse position. A campus postcard sits in front. CSS perspective and extruded text provide depth without a WebGL bundle. The scene is decorative, does not hide required information, and stops responding under reduced motion.

## Composition
The homepage begins with an oversized Mongolian headline on yellow, followed by a blue values band. A quieter school introduction separates the expressive opening from tilted academic panels. News uses straight readable layouts. Admissions and inner-page controls stay conventional and accessible, with the yearbook colors applied consistently.

## Constraints
Keep original data adapters, backend routes, admissions behavior, and admin flows. Use real school-owned media when available; currently the photograph and logo are inherited, with provenance documented in README. Sample school claims remain explicitly labeled. Maintain visible focus, mobile menu focus trapping, keyboard news dialogs and year navigation. The art may tilt; readable content and form fields must not.

## Implementation
Global foundation: src/app/globals.css. Original public layout baseline: src/app/redesign.css. Current visual direction: src/app/yearbook.css. The latter is the authority for colors, typography, composition and component appearance. Pointer interaction lives in src/components/site/school-sculpture.tsx.
